When you are working in the garden, please leave the gate open, when you leave, please close, lock, and spin the lock.

Batteries for the blower and weed wacker. If the batteries are getting low, please bring to Shaun’s house and get the replacement. He always keeps a hot battery to be used at the

Some suggested using hay, pine needles for the bottom layers, and leaves for making mulch. The top layer of the garden bins would be topsoil. Also suggested to use cardboard for the base of the garden bins.
