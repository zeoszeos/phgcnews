From the Archives — 1984

The club's scanned records show a well-organized club more than 40 years ago. The April 5, 1984 agenda has roll call of officers, the treasurer's report, a dinner committee report, and nominations for the 1984–85 season: Charlie Elmer for President and Millard Ashley for Vice President. The 1984 Annual Dinner gave out garden awards chosen by three judges. It also gave tongue-in-cheek "Weed Garden" awards, including a "dis-honorable mention."

Source: NAS "Scanned Archive Docs" (scanned May 6, 2022). Full scanned set: Garden Club Archive Docs (PDF, Wix). The Wix title says the docs go back to 1983. The earliest date read so far is 1984; the pages haven't all been read yet.

Palm Hill Garden Club Rules

  1. All gardens should be kept weeded and in presentable conditions at all times during the garden season and other times if possible. If a member leaves the area for any extended time period or goes north for the summer, the lot must be covered with plastic from border to border. Failure to do so may result in the forfeiture of the garden plot.
  2. A square wire fence or equivalent with a maximum of a one (1) inch opening and eighteen (18) inches high is to be placed and maintained along the fence line.
  3. Watering should be kept at a minimum. Try to regulate the sprinkling so as not to use unnecessary water. Confine watering to your own lot. Watering must be done with a hand-held hose with a shut off valve. Sprinklers must have timers. Watering must be hand held during water restrictions.
  4. Nothing is to be placed or stored along the South and West Fence that borders the Water Company.
  5. Periodic meetings will be held as needed; the time, date and location to be announced.
  6. Members will keep the same plots assigned upon joining the Garden Club. When a member gives up the plot, it reverts to the Garden Club who will reassign it, i.e., it is not just given to another member.
  7. The above regulations are subject to change, deletion and/or addition at any regular meeting by a majority vote of the membership, after one month’s notice.
  8. Garden Club members who do not abide by these regulations may be suspended for cause by a vote of the members in good standing after notification.
  9. Dogs or pets of any kind are not allowed in the Palm Hill Garden area.

Palm Hill Garden Club Bylaws

SECTION 1 NAME: The name of this organization shall be "The Palm Hill Garden Club" and it may be referred to as "The Club".

SECTION 2 LOCATION: The Palm Hill Garden Club shall be located in the Palm Hill Country Club Mobil Home Park, Largo, Florida, including both the North and South Areas, and shall be till otherwise determined by the membership use the address of the President of the Club as the Official Address of the Club.

ARTICLE II: PURPOSE & OBJECTIVES

The purpose and objectives of the Palm Hill Garden Club shall be to provide a medium for information, cooperation, and fellowship among those Palm Hill residents who actively participate in the Vegetable Garden programs on the lots furnished by courtesy of the Park Management. Also, to sponsor programs relating to gardening in general which may be of interest to the members and to other Palm Hill Residents.

ARTICLE III: MEMBERSHIP

SECTION 1 Active Membership in the Club shall be comprised of Palm Hill residents who maintain an active garden project in the Vegetable Garden program sponsored by the Palm Hill Garden Club.

SECTION 2 Charter Members shall be those participating in the garden project at the time these bylaws are adopted.

SECTION 3 Active members other than charter members shall be any Park resident who has been assigned a garden lot and who applies for and is approved by Club Membership.

SECTION 4 Associate Member or Honorary Member may be approved by the Club Membership.

SECTION 5 The term membership shall be for one year from April 1 to April 1 unless otherwise voted by the Membership.

SECTION 6 Membership Dues and Assessment, if any, shall be determined by vote of the membership.

SECTION 7 Members may be suspended, for cause, by vote of the members in good standing.

SECTION 8 A "Member in Good Standing" shall mean any member whose membership dues or assessment are paid up-to-date and who is not in violation of any of the "rules" which have been established by the Club.

ARTICLE IV: OFFICERS AND DIRECTORS:

SECTION 1 The Palm Hill Garden Chub shall function under the direction of a Board of Directors and Officers duly elected by the Club Membership, as follows:

A Board of Directors of five members including the President, Vice President, Secretary/Treasurer, and three Directors at large.

SECTION 2 Election of Officers and Directors: All officers and directors shall be regular active members in good standing, and except for those first elected under these bylaws, shall be elected at the Annual Meeting of the Club by the majority vote of the membership present.

SECTION 3 Officers and Directors shall serve for a term of one (1) year, or until their successors are duly elected and qualified, or unless suspended under the provisions of ARTICLE II, SECTION 7.

SECTION 4 The first officers and directors to be elected shall be nominated by a special committee of three (3) to be named by the temporary chairman. Those eligible for charter membership in the Club shall be eligible to vote on the adoption of these bylaws and on the election of the first officers and directors.

ARTICLE V: DUTIES OF OFFICERS

SECTION 1 The president shall preside at all meetings and appoint all standing committees and such special committees as may be deemed necessary. He shall be ex-officio member of any and all committees, and shall have a voice and vote therein. He shall have power, with approval of the Board, to fill any and all elective offices by appointment when such office is vacated for any reason. Any such appointees shall serve during the unexpired term of the office.

SECTION 2 The vice president shall assist the president in the performance of his duties and, in the absence of the president, shall assume the duties of the president.

SECTION 3 The secretary-treasurer shall keep accurate minutes of all meetings unless otherwise provided for, and shall perform all other duties that may pertain to the office of secretary-treasurer as may be requested from time to time by the president. At the time of expiration of his term of office, he shall deliver all the books, papers, records, and all other things belonging to the Club, to the Board of Directors.

SECTION 4 The secretary-treasurer shall also keep account of all monies of the Club. He shall keep suitable account books and all other papers pertaining to club finances and pay all bills incurred by the Club, approved by the Board of Directors. He shall report to the membership, when requested by the Board of Directors, the receipts and expenditures of the Club, and submit his records for audit at the Annual Meeting.

SECTION 5 By a majority of the Board of Directors, any officer or director may be removed from office for failure to fulfill the duties of his office.

ARTICLE VI: POWERS OF DIRECTORS, QUORUM AND MEETINGS

SECTION 1 The Board of Directors shall have supervision over and charge of the business and financial affairs of the Club and shall authorize appropriation of its funds with approval of a majority of the members present and voting at a duly called meeting.

SECTION 2 The Board shall also have authority to adopt and enforce necessary rules and regulations governing the Operation of the garden project.

SECTION 3 Lot assignments shall be made by a committee of the president and two other members of his selection.

SECTION 4 A majority of the members of the Board shall constitute a quorum.

SECTION 5 Meetings of the Board of Directors shall be held upon call of the president or by written request of a majority of the directors.

ARTICLE VII: MEMBERSHIP MEETINGS

Meetings of the Club membership shall be held upon call of the president or by a majority of the Board of Directors, or by a signed notice of 10 of the members. 50% of the membership shall constitute a quorum.

ARTICLE VIII: ANNUAL MEETING

The annual meeting of the Club shall be held in April of each year, at a time and place to be determined by the Board of Directors. Not less than 10 days’ notice of such meeting shall be given the membership by the president.

ARTICLE IX: ANNUAL ELECTION

SECTION 1 Officers and directors for the coming year shall be elected at the annual meeting of the Club in the month of April, unless otherwise determined by the membership.

SECTION 2 Nominations for officers and directors shall be made by a nominating committee of three members appointed by the president. He shall announce the members of such committee not less than 20 days prior to the date of the annual meeting.

SECTION 3 The nominating committee shall make its report to the president no later than five (5) days prior to the date of the annual meeting.

SECTION 4 In addition to the nominations made be the committee, nominations may be made from the floor by any regular member in good standing. No member's name may be placed in nomination for any office without his or her consent.

SECTION 5 Officers and Directors shall be installed and take office immediately following their election at the annual meeting.

ARTICLE X: AMENDMENTS TO BYLAWS

SECTION 1 Amendments to the Club bylaws may be recommended by a majority of the Board of Directors or 25% of the current membership in good standing.

SECTION 2 Amendments may be made by 51% vote of the members present at a meeting of the membership, provided that notice be made to the membership of such meeting and of the intent to consider amendments to the bylaws.

ARTICLE XI DISPOSITION OF FUNDS

In the event that the Club shall fail to continue to function or should be discontinued, all the moneys remaining in the treasury shall be disposed of in a manner approved by a majority vote of the Board of Directors.
